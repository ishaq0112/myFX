Quickstart

Send your key in the X-API-Key header.

GET /v1/latest
curl -H "X-API-Key: myfx_live_…" "/v1/latest?base=USD"
GET /v1/convert
curl -H "X-API-Key: myfx_live_…" "/v1/convert?from=USD&to=INR&amount=100"
GET /v1/currencies
curl -H "X-API-Key: myfx_live_…" "/v1/currencies"
